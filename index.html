Screen Recording (shows mouse movement)

A web page can't track your mouse outside its own window — that's true even if you pick "Entire screen"; browsers never give pages raw system cursor coordinates. The cursor is still visible in the video itself though, so movement/clicks show up in playback.

Your microphone is captured whenever Tutorial mode is on, or whenever you share "Entire screen". Sound already playing on your screen (e.g. a video) is captured too if your browser's share dialog offers a "Share audio" checkbox — tick it there; this is a browser-level option, not something this app can turn on for you. Chrome/Edge on Windows support this for Entire Screen, a Window, and a Chrome/Edge Tab.
